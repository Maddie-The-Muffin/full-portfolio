## Background

As I'm building out my portfolio, I'm looking to make apps based around things I enjoy, and Book Tracker covers one of those - reading. Some of the inspiration for the project came from my cousin showing me a really neat reading tracking app she had on her phone. Book Tracker is an app designed to help people keep track of the books they've read over time, as well as to encourage them to think about what they've read (with the help of the post-book reflection questions).

## Challenges and solutions

The most difficult thing I faced was implementing and parsing the query string for the reading stats on the homepage. As is the case with things one has never done before, I couldn't wrap my head around the structure of it, so I looked at examples from this project (the same pattern is used on the shelf page) and online. With those examples, I was able to figure out how to implement the parsing of a query string.

## What went right

The "Reading Stats" section I created on my own, with no help from AI. I wanted to demonstrate that I understood the technologies used in this project, hence my determination to program it fully by hand.

## What went wrong

I didn't think about implementing authentication/authorization for the project until late in its lifetime and that created unnecessary work and stress. Next time I will be more cognizant of whether a project will need authentication/authorization when planning it.
