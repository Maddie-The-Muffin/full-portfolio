## Background

This is a website built for the indie games PR company Ad Astra Consulting, with the goal of marketing its services to potential clients. At the start of the project, I sat down with the client and we went over what features he wanted the website to have. I scaffolded the basics and showed him the rough draft of the site structure. After reworking the structure with him, I used Claude Code to add Tailwind styling to the site, based on the space/astronomy theme the client wished for. Certain details such as the constellations and the "Book with Calendly" link we iterated many times until he deemed it perfect.

## Challenges and solutions

The constellations were the most difficult thing to get right. It started off as just one constellation in the background that had a line connect the stars as the user scrolled. We went from that to a curved constellation, back to the sharp-angled constellation, to two constellations on opposite corners, and finally the three constellations you see now. The solution with something like this is always iteration and constant communication with the client.

## What went right

Creating the Astro components I needed (including the project cards and services fold-outs) before proceeding with the restyling made that process much easier. It also helped me get a rough structure up sooner for the client's review.

## What went wrong

I can be a perfectionist, so when I present something to someone and they ask me to tweak it, the more it happens, the worse I feel. I feel as if I've let the client down by not getting it right on the first try. In the tech world, where code changes and updates need to be made all the time, revisions are something I just have to get used to.
