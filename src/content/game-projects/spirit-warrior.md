## Background

This game was made with my husband and myself for Game Maker's Toolkit 2021 game jam. The theme was "join together"; our concept was a spirit warrior that could detach his spirit from his body and fight with either separately or both at once. Each form gives you different strengths and weaknesses (e.g. spirit being able to go through walls; body having more health).

## My role

I handled all of the programming for the game, as well as the environment art.

## What went right

I'm not super happy with much about this project (see "What went wrong") but one thing I am proud of is the environment tileset.

## What went wrong

I was so focused on making the game look pretty that gameplay fell far to the wayside and as a result, the game turned out not fun at all (and buggy!).
